# Check what drives the prop

Use the league or organizer for the schedule, entrant and game log. Use the team's official availability report or lineup and credible local reporting for role changes. Pick the items that can actually change the selected leg.

| Sport | Opportunity and matchup | Definition to verify |
|---|---|---|
| American or Canadian football | Starter, snap and route share, carries or targets, offensive line, opponent and outdoor wind | Rushing vs scrimmage yards, passing vs scoring a TD, overtime and participation |
| Basketball | Confirmed starters, injury designations, minutes, usage, rotation and back-to-back rest | Full game or period, regulation or overtime, single stat vs combined stats |
| Baseball or softball | Probable or confirmed pitcher, opener or bulk role, pitch limit, lineup position, handedness, park and weather | Pitcher start requirements, plate appearances, shortened games and doubleheader game number |
| Soccer | Starting XI, expected minutes, penalty and set-piece roles, rotation and match state | Regulation plus stoppage time vs extra time, shots vs shots on target, substitute and postponement rules |
| Ice hockey | Starting goalie, line and power-play assignment, time on ice, schedule and opponent shot volume | Shots on goal vs attempts, goalie participation, overtime or shootout |
| Tennis | Surface, best-of format, recent workload, serve and return profile, physical concerns | Match vs set, completion, retirements, walkovers and tiebreak scoring |
| Cricket | Match format, confirmed XI, batting position or bowling overs, pitch, toss and rain | Innings vs match, reduced overs, DLS and participation |
| Rugby or Australian rules | Code, squad and starter role, minutes, goal-kicking duties, venue and conditions | Try vs point, player exclusion, regulation, extra time and stat provider |
| Combat sports | Confirmed bout and weigh-in, scheduled rounds, style, late opponent changes | Method and round definitions, draws, no contests and totals at a half-round |
| Golf or motorsport | Field or grid, course or track, format, qualifying and weather | Round vs tournament or race, starting status, cuts, finish classification and dead heats |
| Esports | Title, patch, roster or substitutes, opponent, map pool and series format | Map vs series props, remakes, forfeits and required participation |

For another sport, identify its scoring unit, competition format, official statistic, participation trigger and predictor of opportunity. Follow the same evidence and pricing checks. If the venue offers only team or match markets, use those only when the request permits them.
